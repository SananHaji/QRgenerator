What you can do

  • Add your logo or any picture to the center and set its size and shape.
  • Put a photo behind the code; the corner eyes stay readable so it still scans.
  • Pick colors for the code, eye frames, eye centers and background, including transparent.
  • Choose shapes: square, rounded, circle, diamond, star or line pixels; square, rounded or circle eyes.
  • Save a 1024 × 1024 PNG for print, or share it straight to WhatsApp, Instagram or email.
  • Any language: Cyrillic, Arabic and Azerbaijani text is encoded correctly.

Questions

How do I make a QR code with a logo on Android?
Open Custom QR, paste your link, tap Choose picture under Center logo, adjust size and shape, then tap Save to gallery.
Will a QR code with a logo or photo still scan?
Yes. Error correction rises automatically to fit the logo, and “Keep eyes readable on the picture” keeps the corner squares high-contrast.
Do the QR codes expire?
No. They are static codes: the content is inside the code, so they work forever without a subscription.
Is it free? Does it have ads?
Free, no ads, no account and no watermark.
What size are the saved codes?
1024 × 1024 PNG in Pictures/QRCodeGenerator.
